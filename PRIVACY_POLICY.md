# Privacy Policy — TradeKnowledge Academy

**Last updated: [6/10/2026]**




This Privacy Policy explains how **[Grant horn]** ("we", "us") handles
information when you use the **TradeKnowledge Academy** mobile app (the "App").

## Who we are

TradeKnowledge Academy is an educational app for studying the skilled trades
(electrical, HVAC, and plumbing) and preparing for trade exams and certifications.

Contact: **[tradeknowledgeacademy@gmail.com]**

## Information we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account, we collect your **email
address** and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider
(Supabase) and are stored in hashed form — we never see your plain-text password.

**Learning activity.** To save your progress across devices, we store data tied to
your account, such as lessons completed, quiz results, points, streaks, and levels.

**Purchase information.** If you buy a course or subscription, the transaction is
processed by **Google Play** and managed through **RevenueCat**. We receive a record
that an entitlement (such as "all-access" or a specific course) is active for your
account. **We do not collect or store your credit card or full payment details** —
those are handled by Google Play.

**On-device data.** Some features — Jobsite Notes, the Apprenticeship Hour Tracker,
and your missed-question history — are stored **only on your device** and are not
uploaded to our servers (unless a future version offers optional sync, which would be
disclosed).

**Technical/diagnostic data.** Like most apps, basic technical information (such as
crash or error logs) may be generated to keep the App working. 

We do **not** knowingly collect precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone
data, and the App does **not** show third-party ads.

## How we use information

- To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
- To save and sync your learning progress.
- To unlock content you've purchased and keep entitlements accurate.
- To operate, maintain, and improve the App.
- To respond to support requests.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** share it with
advertisers.

## Service providers we use

We rely on a few trusted providers that process data on our behalf:

- **Supabase** — authentication and database (account + progress data).
- **RevenueCat** — subscription/purchase management.
- **Google Play** — app distribution and payment processing.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy terms. 

## Data retention and deletion

We keep account and progress data while your account is active. **You can delete your
account at any time from within the App** (Settings → Delete Account), which removes
your account and associated server-side data. On-device data (notes, hours) is removed
when you delete the app or clear its data. To request deletion another way, email
**[tradeknowledgeacademy@gmail.com]**.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your
personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these
rights, use the in-app deletion option or contact **[tradeknowledgeacademy@gmail.com]**. We will respond
as required by applicable law.

including encryption in transit and hashed passwords. No method of transmission or
